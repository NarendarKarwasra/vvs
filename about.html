The Journey Behind The Voice

Over the years, my voice has become part of some of the most recognised shows in the country. As the narrator of Bigg Boss since 2010, and through shows like Kaun Banega Crorepati, Indian Idol, MasterChef India, and others, I’ve had the opportunity to be a consistent voice across Indian television. Somewhere along the way, voice stopped being just a profession and became a deeper study of identity, of how sound carries intent, authority, and emotion.

In 2018, Acting brought in another dimension. Working across OTT platforms and films, stepping into different characters, and being in front of the camera made one thing very clear that truth cannot be faked. The camera catches everything and that process deepened my understanding of behaviour, emotion, and presence in a way no theory can.

These experiences naturally led me to the third space I now work in - People.

Today, I work with leaders, founders, and professionals across industries, helping them bridge the gap between what they want to say and how they are actually perceived. The work is not about tips or surface-level techniques. It is about alignment of thought, of voice, and of body; especially in high pressure situations where it matters the most.

Alongside this, I continue to work closely with actors and creators on voice and performance, helping them find authenticity in how they sound and show up.

Across all of this, whether it’s a show, a scene, or a boardroom conversation, the challenge remains the same : “You are being heard, but are you being understood?”
